What's included

What we technically do.

🍪

Cookie consent banner deployment

We deploy GDRock's pre-built cookie consent banner script on your site, configure its settings for your cookie categories, and technically verify that non-essential scripts do not fire before the visitor gives consent.

📄

Privacy policy template configuration

We populate GDRock's pre-built privacy policy template with your business details, data processor list, and retention periods — then publish it as a page on your site and configure all required links.

🤝

Processor agreement setup

We identify your active data processors (Shopify, Stripe, Mailchimp, Meta, Google, etc.) and walk you through activating the standard DPA each processor provides to their customers.

🗂️

Data retention template & configuration

We complete your data retention table and, where your platform allows, configure automatic data deletion schedules to match GDPR requirements.

📊

Technical setup summary

After deployment we send you a written technical summary: every component installed, your site's updated scan score, and any remaining configuration steps.
