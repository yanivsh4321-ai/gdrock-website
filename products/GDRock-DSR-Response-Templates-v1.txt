IMPORTANT RULES:
• You have 30 days to respond from the date of receiving the request
• You may extend by a further 60 days for complex requests (notify the person within 30 days)
• Response must be FREE OF CHARGE (unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive)
• Respond in the same language as the request where possible
• Verify the person's identity before sharing or deleting data

Hi {{FIRST_NAME}},

Following your request dated {{REQUEST_DATE}}, please find below
a summary of the personal data we hold about you.

DATA WE HOLD:
• Name: {{NAME}}
• Email address: {{EMAIL}}
• Shipping address: {{ADDRESS}}
• Order history: {{ORDER_DATES_AND_AMOUNTS}}
• Account created: {{DATE}}
• Marketing consent: {{Yes/No — date given: DATE}}

HOW WE USE IT:
• To process and fulfil your orders (lawful basis: Contract)
• To comply with tax/accounting laws (lawful basis: Legal obligation)
• To send you marketing emails if you consented (lawful basis: Consent)

WHO WE SHARE IT WITH:
• Payment processor (Stripe/PayPal) — for payment processing
• Shipping carrier — for order delivery
• Email platform ({{PLATFORM}}) — for order confirmations and marketing

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT:
• Order data: 7 years (statutory requirement)
• Account data: 3 years after last log-in
• Marketing data: until you unsubscribe

YOUR RIGHTS:
You may request correction, deletion, or portability of your data
at any time. Reply to this email to exercise any of these rights.

If you are unhappy with our response, you have the right to lodge
a complaint with your national data protection authority.
