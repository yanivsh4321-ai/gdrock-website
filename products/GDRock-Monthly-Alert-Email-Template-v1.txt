TIPS FOR WRITING A GOOD UPDATE:
- Keep it under 300 words total
- Always reference a real enforcement action — it creates urgency
- The tip should be something they can do in under 30 minutes
- End with a clear way to contact you
- Send on the 1st or 2nd of each month for consistency
