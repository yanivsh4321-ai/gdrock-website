The questions agencies ask.

Is the pilot really free? What's the catch?

Really free — up to 20 client sites, installed and maintained for 14 days, no card on file. The catch is honest: we're selecting five founding agencies, and a portfolio that has seen GDRock working for two weeks is our best sales pitch. On day 12 you either continue on portfolio pricing or we uninstall everything.

What access do you need to our client sites?

One script tag in each site's <head>. You can paste it yourself, give us temporary CMS access, or mix both. We never touch themes, checkouts or anything beyond the consent layer, and every change is listed in the per-site report.

Is it genuinely white-label?

Yes. The banner is styled per client brand, the compliance reports carry your agency's name, and your clients are your clients — GDRock invoices you, never them.

Do you sign a DPA?

Yes — a GDPR Art. 28 Data Processing Agreement is part of onboarding. You can read it before you ever send us a site: gdrock.com/dpa.

Which platforms do you cover?

Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, Webflow, Wix, BigCommerce and custom stacks — anything that can load a script tag. Mixed portfolios are normal; that's exactly what the pilot is for.

How is this cheaper than per-domain pricing?

Per-site consent tools price each domain like a separate customer. GDRock prices the portfolio as one relationship — that's how €299/month covers 25 sites, and why the per-site cost drops as you grow rather than climbing.

Who is liable if a client is fined?

The data controller is — your client — exactly as with any consent tool. GDRock provides software and templates, not legal advice or certification. What we remove is the implementation gap: the banner that was never installed and the policy that was never updated.

What happens to our clients if GDRock stops?

A fair question for an early company. The documents are yours to keep permanently, the install is a single script tag you can remove in seconds, and consent logs can be exported per site. You are never locked in by data you can't take with you.
