Every fine on this ledger was avoidable.

€4.5 billion in GDPR penalties since 2018, against businesses of every size. Nearly every case traces back to the same four mistakes.

  1. 01

    No real cookie banner

    A notice bar isn't consent. GDPR requires a genuine accept or reject choice before any non-essential cookie loads.

  2. 02

    Outdated privacy policy

    Copy-pasted policies fail. Yours must name your actual processors, retention periods and lawful bases — and be easy to find.

  3. 03

    No breach response plan

    You get 72 hours to notify regulators after a breach. Most stores have no plan and miss the window.

  4. 04

    No data retention schedule

    You can't keep customer data forever. Every data type needs a defined retention period.

Questions, answered.

What exactly is in Core Pack?

A white-label privacy policy template, cookie banner script, data retention table, breach response pack, and a Shopify setup guide — all pre-configured for EU compliance.

Is the cookie banner GDPR-compliant?

Here is what it does. Non-essential cookies stay blocked until a visitor opts in, every choice is logged with a timestamp, and visitors can accept all, reject all, or choose category by category. Whether a particular site is compliant also depends on how it is set up and what else it runs, so we don't certify compliance.

How do I install the cookie banner?

Add one script tag to your website head. No plugins, no complex setup. Our guide walks you through exactly where to paste it.

Do I need to edit the templates?

Minimal editing only — you replace your store name, email, and contact details. Everything else is pre-configured.

Core Pack is a one-time €29 purchase: you get the documents and the banner script and you install them yourself. Care is €15/month and we host and maintain it for you — when EU law changes, your banner updates without you touching anything. Core Pack does not include hosting or updates.

Do you cover non-EU businesses selling to EU customers?

Yes. If you collect personal data from EU residents, GDPR applies to you regardless of where your business is based.
