      What regulators fined, and why.

      Ten published decisions from European and UK regulators, and the gap behind each one. Small stores have the same four gaps.

      1. 01

        No real cookie banner

        A notice bar isn't consent. GDPR requires a genuine accept or reject choice before any non-essential cookie loads.

      2. 02

        Outdated privacy policy

        Copy-pasted policies fail. Yours must name your actual processors, retention periods and lawful bases — and be easy to find.

      3. 03

        No breach response plan

        If a breach puts people at risk, the regulator must hear within 72 hours. A plan written in advance is how you make it.

      4. 04

        No data retention schedule

        You can't keep customer data forever. Every data type needs a defined retention period.

      Is the cookie banner GDPR-compliant?

      Here is what it does. Non-essential cookies stay blocked until a visitor opts in, visitors can accept all, reject all, or choose category by category, and with Care every choice is logged with a timestamp. Whether a particular site is compliant also depends on how it is set up and what else it runs, so we don't certify compliance.
