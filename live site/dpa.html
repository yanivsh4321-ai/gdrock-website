This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) governs how GDRock Ltd processes personal data on behalf of its customers, in accordance with Article 28 of the GDPR.

Data Processing Agreement — GDRock Ltd

How to execute this DPA

This DPA forms part of the agreement between you (the Controller) and GDRock Ltd (the Processor) and takes effect on acceptance of our Terms or use of the Services. A countersigned copy is available on request — see section 9 below.

1. Parties

  • Controller: The customer (the GDRock subscriber).
  • Processor: GDRock Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (registration pending), with registered address in London, United Kingdom.

2. Subject Matter

GDRock processes personal data on behalf of the Controller solely to provide the GDRock compliance platform and associated services.

3. Nature and Purpose of Processing

Hosting cookie consent banners, logging consent records, storing privacy policy versions, and providing compliance reports.

4. Type of Personal Data Processed

Website visitor consent records (anonymised where possible), IP addresses (where consent logging requires), and email addresses of data subjects who submit requests.

5. Duration

For the term of the subscription plus 30 days for data export, then deletion.

6. Processor Obligations (GDRock Ltd)

  • Process data only on documented instructions from the Controller
  • Ensure confidentiality obligations on all staff
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures per GDPR Art. 32
  • Not engage sub-processors without prior written consent (general authorisation granted for those listed below)
  • Assist Controller with data subject rights requests
  • Delete or return all data on termination
  • Provide audit cooperation on reasonable notice

7. Approved Sub-Processors

  • Supabase (data storage) — EU region
  • Vercel (hosting and infrastructure) — EU region where available
  • Zoho (payment processing)
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER PLACEHOLDER]

8. Controller Obligations

Controller warrants it has lawful basis to instruct processing and will not instruct GDRock to process data in violation of applicable law.

9. Contact

To request a countersigned DPA, email legal@gdrock.com with subject line "DPA Request — [your company name]". We will return a signed copy within 5 business days.
