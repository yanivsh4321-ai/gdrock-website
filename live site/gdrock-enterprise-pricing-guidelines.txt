SECTION 1 — COMPANY & PRODUCT OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

GDRock is a B2B SaaS compliance infrastructure platform built for
EU-regulated digital businesses. Headquartered and operated under EU
jurisdiction, GDRock provides automated GDPR compliance tooling,
Supabase-powered data-consent storage vaults, and custom framework
implementation services to SaaS companies, e-commerce operators,
and AI platforms processing personal data of EU residents.

All products and services are delivered as software assets,
pre-configured digital templates, and technical implementation
engagements. GDRock does not provide legal advice or act as a
data protection officer or legal representative. Customers retain
full legal responsibility for their own compliance obligations.

Merchant of Record: All transactions are processed by
Paddle.com Market Ltd. GDRock operates as the product and
service provider under Paddle's reseller model.

Contact: office@gdrock.com

Payment processor: Paddle.com Market Ltd.


SECTION 2 — CORE PRODUCT PORTFOLIO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

GDRock's infrastructure platform comprises three primary
service categories:

  A. AUTOMATED EU COMPLIANCE TRACKING SCRIPTS
     Custom-coded JavaScript and server-side scripts that monitor,
     capture, and log consent events, cookie categorisation states,
     and data subject interactions across web properties. Scripts
     are deployed directly to customer infrastructure (Vercel,
     Netlify, custom servers) and operate in real time, triggering
     webhooks and feeding structured consent records into the
     customer's Supabase vault.

  B. SUPABASE DATA-CONSENT STORAGE VAULTS
     Purpose-built Supabase database schemas, Row Level Security
     (RLS) policies, and edge function pipelines that store
     granular, timestamped consent records in compliance with
     GDPR Article 7 accountability requirements. Each vault is
     configured to the customer's specific data categories,
     processing purposes, and retention schedules. Customers
     receive full ownership and access to their vault data.

  C. CUSTOM FRAMEWORK IMPLEMENTATION
     End-to-end deployment of GDRock's compliance framework
     tailored to the customer's technology stack. This includes
     configuration of Data Processing Agreements, Records of
     Processing Activities (RoPA), Legitimate Interests
     Assessments (LIA), and multi-jurisdiction policy
     documentation for businesses operating across multiple
     EU member states or handling cross-border AI data flows.

SECTION 4 — INCLUDED ENTERPRISE SERVICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All Enterprise engagements (both Scale and Custom tiers) include
the following services, with Custom Enterprise Architecture
receiving expanded scope as noted:


  4.1  DEDICATED DATA-MAPPING PIPELINES
       Custom ETL pipelines mapping the customer's personal data
       flows from collection point to storage, processing, and
       deletion. Delivered as documented architecture diagrams and
       Supabase schema configurations aligned to the customer's
       RoPA obligations.

  4.2  CUSTOM SUPABASE WEBHOOKS
       Real-time webhook integrations triggering on consent grant,
       consent withdrawal, and data subject access requests. These
       webhooks connect GDRock's consent vault to the customer's
       own application layer, enabling automated compliance
       responses without manual intervention.

  4.3  PRIORITY 24/7 SLA SUPPORT
       Dedicated technical support with guaranteed response times:
       · Critical issues (consent system down): 2-hour response
       · High priority (misconfiguration detected): 8-hour response
       · Standard queries: 24-hour response
       A named GDRock technical account manager is assigned to
       each Enterprise client for the duration of the engagement.

  4.4  LIABILITY-SHIELD DOCUMENTATION PACKAGE
       A structured set of pre-configured legal and operational
       documents designed to demonstrate accountability under
       GDPR Articles 5(2) and 24. Includes:
       · Signed Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with all active
         processors identified during onboarding
       · Record of Processing Activities (RoPA) completed and
         formatted for regulatory submission
       · Data Breach Response Protocol template (Articles 33–34)
       · Privacy Impact Assessment (DPIA) template for high-risk
         processing activities
       NOTE: These documents are pre-configured software templates.
       They are not legal advice and do not constitute a guarantee
       of regulatory compliance. Customers are advised to review
       all documentation with qualified legal counsel.

  4.5  AUTOMATED EU COMPLIANCE TRACKING SCRIPTS
       Custom-coded consent tracking scripts deployed to the
       customer's infrastructure. Scripts log every consent
       interaction with a cryptographic timestamp, user
       identifier hash, consent category, and consent version
       reference — creating an immutable audit trail.

  4.6  SUPABASE DATA-CONSENT STORAGE VAULT
       A dedicated, customer-owned Supabase project configured
       with GDRock's consent schema, RLS policies, and automated
       retention/purge logic. The vault is hosted in an EU-region
       Supabase instance (Frankfurt or Ireland) to satisfy GDPR
       data residency requirements.

  4.7  QUARTERLY COMPLIANCE REVIEW CALLS (Custom Tier Only)
       Four scheduled 60-minute review calls per year with a
       GDRock technical lead. Reviews cover: consent system
       health, new regulatory developments affecting the
       customer's data processing, script performance metrics,
       and vault data integrity verification.
