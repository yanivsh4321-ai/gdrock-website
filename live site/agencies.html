The business case

Compliance as a profit line, not a favour.

Most agencies handle GDPR for clients ad hoc — a plugin here, a policy template there, unbilled hours everywhere. GDRock flips that: you resell compliance under your own brand and bill it like hosting.

Charge each client a monthly compliance fee. GDRock costs you a flat portfolio price. The difference is margin on work we do — installs, maintenance, law-change updates, consent logs, all of it.

And when a regulator or a nervous client asks, you hand over timestamped consent logs and a per-site report with your agency's name on it.

What every client site gets

The full GDPR layer, per site.

Hosted consent banner

One script tag. Accept-all, reject-all and granular choices, styled to each client's brand.

Pre-consent tracker blocking

GA4, Meta Pixel and marketing tags stay blocked until the visitor opts in — the violation regulators check first.

Timestamped consent logs

Audit-ready records of every consent event, exportable per client when anyone asks for proof.

Auto-updates on law changes

When EU rules move, every banner and policy across the portfolio updates — no redeploys, no tickets.

Policy templates

Privacy policy, cookie policy and retention tables — white-label, pre-configured, published per site.

Continuous scanning

Every site re-checked with the GDRock scanner, so a client's new marketing tag never becomes your problem.
