Cookie Policy — GDRock

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to site operators. Some technologies, such as browser local storage, work similarly to cookies by storing small amounts of data in your browser; this policy covers those too.

3. Third-Party Cookies

GDRock's consent banner is first-party and is designed to block all non-essential and third-party cookies until you give consent. At present, this website does not load any third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce an analytics tool in future (for example Google Analytics 4), it will only run after you opt in via the consent banner, and we will update this policy to name it.

We load web fonts from Google Fonts to style the site. This does not set advertising cookies, but it does involve a request to Google's font servers.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can manage your preferences at any time using our consent banner, or through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear local storage via their privacy or security settings.
