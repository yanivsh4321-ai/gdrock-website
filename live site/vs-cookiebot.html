The portfolio math

Per-site pricing is the agency tax.

Cookiebot's model — per domain, priced by subpage count — is fair for a business running its own site. Free for one domain up to 50 subpages. Premium plans run from roughly €7/month (small sites) to €90/month per domain for large ones, and Cookiebot roughly doubled its base Premium price in August 2025 — and every domain in a portfolio is its own subscription.

For an agency, that same model multiplies: every new client adds another subscription, another setup, another thing your team maintains without billing for it.

GDRock prices the portfolio as one relationship — and the flat price includes the labour: installs, verification, consent logs, law-change updates, and white-label reports your clients can be shown.
