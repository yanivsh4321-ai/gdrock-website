The problem

Most online stores are non-compliant right now.

GDPR has been enforced since 2018. Regulators are actively fining businesses of all sizes — and the mistakes are almost always the same four things.

🍪

No proper cookie banner

A notification bar is not enough. GDPR requires a real accept/reject choice before any non-essential cookies load.

📄

Outdated privacy policy

Copy-pasted policies don't cut it. Your policy must name your actual processors, retention periods, and lawful bases.

⏱

No breach response plan

GDPR requires you to notify regulators within 72 hours of a data breach. Most businesses have no plan.

🗃

No data retention schedule

You can't keep customer data forever. GDPR requires defined retention periods for every data type.
