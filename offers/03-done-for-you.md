## Who it is for

Someone who will not open a file. They have revenue, they do not have time, and
they would rather pay to have it done than learn what a script tag is.

This is the offer where you sell your hands, and it is the highest-value thing you
can do per hour right now.

## Why the prices went up

Old prices were €199/€499/€999. They were set against plugin prices instead of
against service value. An agency bills roughly €45/site/month for compliance;
€999 for multi-day SaaS work was a discount nobody had asked for. Volume here is
low by design — the constraint is your hours, so the price has to reflect that.

## What it explicitly does NOT include

- **No legal sign-off.** You are not a law firm, and the site says so. You
  implement; you do not certify.
- **Not ongoing** unless they also take Care. Say this at the point of sale or
  you will be doing free maintenance in month three.
- **No guarantee against a fine.** Ever. Do not let a buyer talk you into implying it.

## Economics

The important number is your hours, not the margin.

| Tier | Price | Realistic hours | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic €249 | €249 | 2–3 | ~€90–125/h |
| Shopify €599 | €599 | 5–7 | ~€85–120/h |
| SaaS €1,500 | €1,500 | 12–16 | ~€95–125/h |

If any tier drops below about €80/hour in practice, raise the price rather than
working faster.
