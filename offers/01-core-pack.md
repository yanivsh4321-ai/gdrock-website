## Who it is for

The solo store owner who is comfortable pasting a script tag and editing a
document, and who wants to spend €29 once rather than subscribe to anything.

Typical profile: Shopify or WooCommerce, under €500k revenue, no legal budget, has
just seen a bad scanner score and wants it gone this afternoon.

## What they get

| Deliverable | Detail |
|---|---|
| White-label privacy policy | Template they fill with store name, email, contact details |
| Cookie banner script | Self-hosted script they paste themselves |
| Data retention table | Per-data-type retention schedule to complete |
| Breach response pack | The 72-hour notification procedure and who to contact |
| Shopify setup guide | Exactly where to paste, per theme |
| Keep it forever | No renewal, no expiry |

## What it explicitly does NOT include

State these plainly — this is the boundary that stops Core Pack eating Care:

- **No hosting.** The banner runs from their own site, not GDRock's CDN.
- **No automatic updates.** When EU law changes they get an email notification;
  updating the documents is on them.
- **No consent log dashboard.** No console access.
- **No support beyond email**, and no install help — that is Done-For-You.

## Time to value

About 10 minutes if they follow the guide. The guide is the product as much as the
templates are.

## Economics

Effectively 100% margin — it is files. The real cost is support email volume from
people who bought a self-install product and wanted an install. Watch that; if it
gets heavy, the answer is to push those buyers to Done-For-You, not to widen
Core Pack.
