currently either ignoring GDPR on them, or paying per domain for a consent tool and
hating it.

## The pitch, which is arithmetic

Agencies bill compliance on to clients at roughly **€45 per site per month**.

| Sites | You pay GDRock | You bill clients | You keep | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | €299/mo | €1,125/mo | **€826/mo** | 73% |
| 50 | €499/mo | €2,250/mo | **€1,751/mo** | 78% |
| 100 | €799/mo | €4,500/mo | **€3,701/mo** | 82% |

Then the structural point, which is the actual wedge:

> Every major consent tool charges **per domain**. Cookiebot is €30 a domain and
> doubled its price in August 2025. At 25 clients that is €750/month and it scales
> against you. GDRock bills the portfolio, not the domain — so your cost per site
> *falls* as you add clients.

## What is honestly not built yet

Say this rather than let them discover it:

- **There is no server-side agency account** that owns 25 client sites. The console
  takes a site parameter and keeps the list per browser. It is real data over a real
  API, but the multi-tenant back end is not built.
- **No client-facing login.** Clients cannot log in to see their own site yet.
- **No case study.** The first Founding Partner pilot is in progress.
- Billing is manual — you invoice, there is no self-serve agency checkout.

None of this blocks a 20-site pilot, which is exactly why the pilot exists. Sell
the pilot, not the platform.

## The pilot offer

**Free for 14 days, up to 20 client sites, first five agencies.**

Purpose is not the €0. It is:
1. Getting your script on 20 real sites so the product gets exercised.
2. Producing the case study you currently don't have.
3. Making the switching cost real before the invoice arrives.

Set the expectation that it converts to a paid portfolio at day 14, in writing, at
the start.

## Economics

At 25 sites you are earning ~€12/site/month for hosting a script and keeping
templates current. Marginal cost is near zero; the real cost is onboarding time on
the first batch. Budget onboarding as a one-off cost of acquiring a multi-year
account, and do it yourself for the first five agencies so you learn what breaks.
